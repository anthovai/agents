Enrolment is the constraint, and it is the number to plan around: a
hundred people starting an exam together are all enrolling at once.

Latency is the reason, not failures: 0 requests failed across all 6 levels,
but p95 went from 0.28s alone to 7.08s at 32 in flight, and a
liveness challenge waiting that long between frames cannot be finished.

Ceiling for the face pipeline only. Moodle, the database, evidence
writes and the browsers are not in this measurement, and the service
was sharing a laptop with all of them while it ran. Treat it as an
order of magnitude, not a capacity plan.
